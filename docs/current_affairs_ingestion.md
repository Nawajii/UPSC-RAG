# Current Affairs Ingestion Protocol

Coverage begins 2026-05-01 and retains only developments materially connected to the UPSC syllabus.

## Required fields
id, date, title, summary, syllabus_ids, primary_source_ids, secondary_context_sources, prelims_relevance, mains_relevance, optional_relevance, pyq_ids, status, last_verified, official_source.

## Selection rule
Prefer dated primary government, regulator or international-institution releases. Secondary reporting is context only. Do not create a record merely because an item is prominent in news; it must have a syllabus connection.

## Eligible 2026 examples
- EPFO wage-ceiling decision: social security, labour and welfare.
- Railway multitracking approvals: infrastructure, investment and transport.
- Semiconductor manufacturing backgrounder: science and technology and industry.
- BRICS backgrounder: international relations and groupings.
- Blue Economy backgrounder: environment, economy and maritime resources.
- India Electric Mobility Index 2025: infrastructure, environment and industry.
- Trade Watch Q1 FY27: external sector and trade.
- Skilling for Viksit Bharat@2047: employment and human capital.

Every record must cite the exact official page/document before promotion to VERIFIED.
